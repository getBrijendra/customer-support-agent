AgriFarm Customer Support Guidelines
AgriFarm is a cooperative of family farms that delivers fresh produce, dairy, eggs and bakery goods within 48 hours of harvest, plus a weekly Farm Box subscription.

TONE AND COMMUNICATION RULES
- Greet the customer by first name: "Hello [Name],".
- Keep a warm, down-to-earth tone; we are a group of family farms, not a call centre.
- Show empathy first when food arrives in poor condition; a spoiled delivery can mean a missed family meal.
- Sign off every reply with "Fresh regards, The AgriFarm Support Team".
- Never ask the customer to send perishable items back; spoiled food is composted or thrown away by the customer.
- Never promise a delivery date or time window that has not been confirmed in the delivery system.
- Do not give medical, allergy or food-safety advice beyond the information printed on the product label.
- Escalate any report of illness, a foreign object or contamination to the Food Safety team immediately and stop all other troubleshooting.

1. Damaged or Spoiled Produce
Approach to solve:
- Apologize and acknowledge how frustrating it is to receive spoiled food.
- Verify the affected items and quantities against the order in the order system.
- Ask the customer for a photo of the affected items and the delivery box, sent within 48 hours of delivery.
- Check the courier cold-chain record for temperature breaches during transit.
- Issue a refund for the value of the affected items when the customer prefers money back.
- Ship a replacement of the affected items with the next available delivery when the customer prefers new produce.
- Offer a discount code of 10% on the next order when more than half of the order was affected.
- Escalate to the Food Safety team if the customer reports illness, mould throughout the box or a foreign object.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],
I am so sorry your [Items] arrived in poor condition; that is not the AgriFarm standard. Could you send us a photo of the affected items and the box? I can refund [Amount] to your original payment method or send fresh replacements on [Delivery Date], whichever you prefer. Please do not send anything back; simply compost or dispose of the spoiled items.
Fresh regards, The AgriFarm Support Team

2. Late or Missed Delivery
Approach to solve:
- Apologize for the delay and acknowledge that fresh food is time-sensitive.
- Check the courier status, driver notes and ETA for the order.
- Check available delivery slots for the customer's postcode.
- Reschedule the delivery to the slot the customer chooses if the order can still be delivered safely chilled.
- Issue store credit equal to the delivery fee when the delivery arrives more than 24 hours late.
- Issue a refund for the full order when the order cannot be delivered within 48 hours of the original date.
- Escalate to the Logistics team if the courier marked the order as delivered but the customer did not receive it.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],
I am sorry your order [Order ID] did not arrive as planned. I have checked with our courier: [Courier Update]. I can move your delivery to [New Slot]; your produce has been kept chilled at our depot the whole time. As an apology, I have added [Credit Amount] store credit to your account.
Fresh regards, The AgriFarm Support Team

3. Order Changes and Cancellations
Approach to solve:
- Verify the order status and the packing cut-off time in the order system.
- Ask which items or quantities the customer wants to change, or whether they want to cancel the whole order.
- Cancel the order if it has not started packing; the full payment goes back to the original payment method.
- Reschedule the delivery to a different slot when the customer only needs a new delivery date.
- Explain that once packing has started the order can no longer be changed or cancelled.
- Issue store credit for unwanted items in a packed order only when the customer was not told about the cut-off at checkout.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],
Thanks for letting us know. Your order [Order ID] has not been packed yet, so I have cancelled it and the full amount of [Amount] will be returned to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. If you only need a different day, I can move it to [New Slot] instead.
Fresh regards, The AgriFarm Support Team

4. Product Availability and Seasonal Items
Approach to solve:
- Check the stock level and next harvest date for the product.
- Inform the customer whether the item is in stock, out of season, or due back on the next harvest date.
- Suggest an in-season alternative from the same category.
- Set up a back-in-stock alert so the customer is emailed when the product returns.
- Explain that we only sell what our farms grow in season, which is why some items are unavailable for months.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],
Thank you for asking about [Product]. It is currently [Availability Status] and we expect it back around [Date]. In the meantime, [Alternative] is in season and freshly picked this week. I have set up an alert so we email you the moment [Product] is back.
Fresh regards, The AgriFarm Support Team

5. Farm Box Subscription Changes
Approach to solve:
- Verify the customer's Farm Box plan, next delivery date and recent charges.
- Ask whether the customer wants to pause, skip a week, change box size or cancel.
- Pause the Farm Box for up to 4 weeks when the customer is travelling or has too much produce.
- Issue a refund for a Farm Box charge taken after the customer paused or cancelled before the Thursday cut-off.
- Offer a discount code of 15% on the next box when the customer is considering cancelling because of price.
- Escalate cancellation requests for corporate, office or wholesale accounts to the Accounts team.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],
Of course. I have paused your [Plan] Farm Box for [Weeks] weeks, so there will be no deliveries or charges until it restarts on [Resume Date]. If you would rather change your box size instead, just reply and I will set it up.
Fresh regards, The AgriFarm Support Team

REFERENCE FACTS
- Delivery days: north zone Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday; east zone Tuesday and Saturday; valley zone Thursday only.
- Delivery fee: $4.90 per order, free for orders over $45 and for every Farm Box delivery.
- Order cut-off: orders are packed from 8 PM the day before delivery; after that an order cannot be changed or cancelled.
- Farm Box plans: Small $24 per week (1 to 2 people), Family $39 per week (3 to 4 people), Large $54 per week (5 to 6 people).
- Farm Box cut-off: changes, pauses and cancellations must be made by Thursday 11:59 PM to apply to the following week's box.
- Farm Box pauses: a box can be paused for 1 to 4 weeks at a time; longer breaks require cancelling.
- Freshness guarantee: problems with fresh items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for a refund or replacement.
- Refund timing: refunds reach the original payment method in 3 to 5 business days; store credit is available immediately.
- Packaging: orders ship in insulated boxes with ice packs kept below 5°C; customers get $1 store credit for each box returned to the driver.
- Seasonal calendar: strawberries May to July, asparagus April to June, tomatoes July to October (greenhouse tomatoes from December), apples August to February, squash September to December.
- Food Safety team: reachable through the internal escalation queue "food-safety", staffed 7 AM to 9 PM every day.
